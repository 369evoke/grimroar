Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grimroar (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by 8th Flame ("we", "us" or "our"), a business located in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. If you have any questions, contact us at mygrimroar@gmail.com.

In short: Grimroar has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. What you write and the photos you add stay on your device. When you subscribe, Apple handles payment and our subscription provider, RevenueCat, confirms your subscription status.

1. Information that stays on your device

Everything you create in the App is stored only on your device, in the App's local storage. This includes your journal entries, grimoire pages, notes, annotations, altars, favourites, course progress, book cover settings and any photos you add. We do not receive, upload or have access to this information.

Because it is stored only on your device, this information is not backed up by us. If you delete the App, or use Delete account in the App's settings, it is permanently erased from your device. Depending on your device settings, your device's own backups (for example, iCloud Backup) may include the App's data; those backups are managed by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

2. Photos

The App only accesses a photo when you choose to add one (for example, to your altar, journal, a grimoire page or your book cover). Photos you choose are stored on your device as described above. We never receive your photos and the App never browses your photo library on its own.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Grimroar Premium is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Apple's App Store. Apple processes all payments. We never receive your name, payment card details or Apple ID password.

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to manage subscriptions. To confirm whether you have an active subscription and to let you restore purchases, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user identifier created by the App (it is not your name, email or Apple ID);
  • purchase and transaction information from Apple, such as the product purchased, purchase and renewal dates, and subscription status;
  • basic technical information, such as the App version, device operating system version, and App Store country or region.

This information is used only to provide and support your subscription. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf under its own privacy policy, available at revenuecat.com/privacy. RevenueCat is based in the United States, so this information may be processed outside Canada.

4. Fonts

The App displays some typefaces from Google Fonts. When these load, your device connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and basic request information, as with any web request. Google's handling of this information is described in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

5. What we don't do

  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information for advertising.
  • We do not require you to create an account.

6. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the contents of your message. We use these only to reply and help you, and we delete support emails when they are no longer needed.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can view, change or delete everything you create in the App directly on your device, and you can erase all of it with Delete account in settings. Under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and other laws that may apply to you, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you and to ask us to correct or delete it. Because we do not hold your App content, such requests will usually concern support emails or subscription records held by RevenueCat. To make a request, email mygrimroar@gmail.com.

9. Security

Your App content stays on your device and is protected by your device's security, such as your passcode and Face ID. Subscription information is handled by Apple and RevenueCat using industry-standard security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above, and if the changes are significant we will let you know in the App or on this page.

Common questions

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Open the App, tap the gear icon at the top right, and tap Restore purchases. You can also tap Grimroar Premium and then Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions, choose Grimroar and tap Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage subscription inside the App. You keep Premium until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Where is my journal and grimoire stored?

Everything you create stays on your iPhone. There's no account, so we can't see or recover it. If you delete the App, your content is deleted too, unless it's included in your own device backup.

How do I delete my data?

Tap the gear icon, scroll to Your data, and choose Delete all notes, Delete all photos or Delete account. Delete account erases everything Grimroar has stored on your device. It does not cancel a subscription; cancel that in your iPhone's settings as described above.

How do I turn the music off?

Tap the speaker button at the top of the screen, or switch off Ambient music in settings.

How do I skip the opening?

Tap Skip during the opening, or switch off Play the opening each time in settings.
